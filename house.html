Our Pillars

01

Self-expression

Poetry and visual art as living language — a way to feel free again, to feel alive, to dance like the winged beings in the sky.

02

Creativity

The courage to make something new — unique poetic and artistic projects that open doors for emerging creatives.

03

Authenticity

The freedom to be exactly who you are — youth that drips like gold, dared not be sold, kept precious and free.

As a creative company, we produce unique poetry and artistic projects. We are dedicated to creating opportunities for emerging creatives to be showcased and recognized.
