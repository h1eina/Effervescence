Excellence Unveiled

as performed at the Lifting Stripes Initiative Scholarships and Awards Gala, 2025

Before
It all seemed so uncertain.

Secrets were hidden behind curtains
And whispered about in conversations.

You had to watch back doors shut and open
While you were left falling into the overflow.

Before
What you didn’t know
Outweighed what you wanted to
And the heaps of hurdles in front of you
Made it all feel impossible.

Until
Someone walked in
And extended hope unburdened.

Cherished your potential
As if it was their purpose.
Lifted up the throw
To reveal what lie underneath
Allowing you too to see
Who you could be.

Since
You have crossed over that bridge.

And the words of gratitude roll off your tongue
They land on them but also touch those to come.

Equipped with all you’ve gained
You give of yourself
Just as others have done
And prove it is always the right thing
Lifting someone else up.

Because on you many blessings have fallen
And more are surely to come
But imagine,

No one had ever lifted a hand
To carry you up and show you the way.
Looked you in the eyes
And gave you an opportunity.

Told you it was possible
And that you were the possibility.

There would be no chance to form community.

Where in this new light
Right here
You are blooming.

And your excellence once buried

Will remain unveiled.

Effervescence

as published in the HPL Power of the Pen Winning Entries, 2022

Effervescent
A captured essence
Stuck in a bottle
Sent off to sea
I question everyday
When will it be returned to me?

I was once effervescent
When youth dripped off me
Like gold so rare
It could have been sold
But dared not be

It was too precious
It kept me free
Then my freedom was taken,
Stripped away from me
I became a shell of what I used to be
I became still
I became recluse
I had become everything I never wanted to be,
But if only then I knew

Now I sit alone
Staring longingly outside my window
Reminiscing my youth
I am not living it
As I had always promised I would
I promised myself that nothing would ever change me
I would remain curious and carefree
Adventurous and wild
Tamed by no wind
Docile by no mile
But suddenly life changed
Responsibilities came
Ones I could not control
Others I bothered not to
Who else would take on the roles I was taught to?
The more I sit back and think
The more it hurts
I worked and worked and worked
Taking no time to discover myself
To find out who I was
To find out what I did best

I left no room for effervescence

So, when I sit alone and ask
When will it be returned to me,
I have realised, it is simply up to me

Do I want my life to be evergreen?
Do I want it to drip with gold?
With incomparable experiences new and old?

Yes, yes, yes

I want to feel free again
I want to feel alive
I want to be able to dance like the winged beings in the sky

I want to restore my effervescence
Although it never left
I believed it was taken,
but I was the one that had let it drown.

Reclaiming Our Wings

as performed at the Hidden Gems Unveiling, 2025

Women are taught to be angels
Soft and graceful
Sweet and gentle
We take on roles as protectors, nurturers, comforters
Though like angels we are seen as supporting characters

Dressed in all white
Hidden in plain sight
We come to uplift but are rarely uplifted
Unlike angels our wings are clipped

Pigeon-holed
Caged like a bird
Expected to serve unseen
Under pressures that make no sense1
We cannot let the halo fall
We have been taught that imperfection is an imperfect thing
And taught to dim the light within

Though true angels are encompassed with light
They walk with it
Talk with it
And are carried by its might

Angels are fierce warriors
And they are anointed by the divine
Holders of wisdom
They drop gems of knowledge wherever they fly

I am reclaiming my wings
I must fly
In fact, we must all soar
Not only should we all shine
We should all glow
We should be fluorescent
Reflecting every facet that lives inside
Deciding for ourselves the kind of woman we are

1 From the poem, You Are. And you are all women. By Gucci Erasmus

‘In the face of’

as written for the Rhophys Couture International Women’s Day feature

(English Version)

We are closer to liberation
Than previous generations,
Though a movement of suppression
Dares to threaten
The progress intended.

These perceptions of women
Marred by misconceptions,
Blurs our futures
And dampens our spirits
Distracting from our purpose.

The illusion of power
Is only designed to empower
What was once hid,
Because feeling intimidated
Was too much to bear.

But we know who we are
And of what we are capable,
Our strength must overpower the noise.

There is no need to regress,
It is forever ahead.
Anything less is a disservice.

Let us eradicate the notion of reversibility,
The world should be awakened by our audacity.
The right side of history must unfold unselfishly.

So let us march on,
As one beat, together,

In unison.

(French Translation)

Nous sommes plus proche à la liberation
Que les generations précédentes,
Mais un mouvement de répression
Ose menacer
Le progrès desirer.

Ces perceptions des femmes
Défigurer par des idées fausses,
Brouillent notre futur
Et nous laissons découragé
Distrayant de notre objectif.

L’illusion du pouvoir
Est seule conçu pour habiliter
Ce qui était autrefois cacher,
Parce que se sentir intimider
Était trop difficile à endurer.

Mais nous savons qui nous sommes
Et de quoi nous sommes capables,
Notre force doit surmonter le bruit.

Nous ne de devons pas régresser,
C’est toujours à devant.
Tout autre choix serait un mauvais service.

Éliminons la notion de réversibilité,
Le monde devrait être réveiller par notre audacité.
Le bon côté de l’histoire doit se dérouler de manière désintéressée.

Alors marchons,
à un seul battement ensemble,

à l’unisson.

Afro Pick

as performed at the inaugural Excel in Style Community Day, 2025

I pick up my
Afro pick
to pick out the knots bound together by 19 years of living.

I pick up my
Afro pick
and the pick pokes
at the twists, curls and edges
of my Afro.

Picking up my
Afro pick
it helps lift the roots from the pits
into a uniform peak,
an act intentionally reclaiming history.

Everyone knows
Afros
hold memories.

The first time I picked up my
Afro pick
and held onto the balled-up fist
on the end,
I was reminded of the radical revolution
preaching to love what grows naturally
which now allows me to walk proudly
with this blooming crown atop my head.

The love now seen on TV
but first born in the streets,
a revolution that involved reclaiming identity
by reshaping the way black people were once seen.

We are their prayers (Psalm 23)

as featured in Volume 2, Issue 2 of the Afrovescence Visual and Poetry Collection, 2026

We are not only our ancestors’ wildest dreams
We are their fervent prayers
Morning, noon and night
We are a product of their unbreakable faith

In any circumstance
They would still praise
Fall to their knees in surrender
Bow their heads and be cloaked in salvation
Hoping it would extend further down their line of creation

They would pour the holy oil
On the crown and over the home
While singing hymns accented by a tinge of heartache
They longed for the words to ring true
“It is well
it is well with my soul”
And prayed, Lord let it be well with them too

Even at the silent whisper of a midnight call
they would rise underneath the ebony sky
interlace their fingers, and again close their eyes
exhale the pressing matters of their hearts
and cast away all burdens weighing on their minds
trusting to be provided with the comfort, clarity, and courage to survive another day.

For the strength to hop on their feet
Sway to the claps and march to the beats
Hoping God can feel their devotion

Endless recitations daily
Until their tongues were numb
Everlasting believers with their steadfast love
what are we without their prayers,
answered and to come.
